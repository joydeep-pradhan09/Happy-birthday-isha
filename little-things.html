The Little
Things I Notice

Not everything memorable is loud. Sometimes it is a glance, a little detail, a familiar style, or something so small you almost miss it.

The little details in a look.

Some expressions do not need an explanation. They simply become part of the memory — little moments that stay even after the picture is gone.

The little things that stay with me.

The way your hair falls, the way the evening light finds you, and those random little moments that somehow become beautiful memories.

Small details, somehow unforgettable.

The kind of little things that may seem ordinary, but somehow become part of what makes someone recognisable in your memory.
