The Story
So Far

Some stories are not written in chapters. They happen through ordinary days, photographs, conversations and moments that become important later.

CHAPTER 01

Before we knew which moments would matter.

There are always moments that seem completely ordinary while they are happening. Later, they become the ones we remember first.

CHAPTER 02

The ordinary days.

Not every memory needs a big occasion. Sometimes a walk, a conversation or a random afternoon is enough to become part of the story.

CHAPTER 03

The memories that stay.

Photographs have a strange way of keeping time. One image can take you back to an entire feeling, even when the details around it have changed.

CHAPTER 04

Still adding pages.

The story is not finished. There are still new places, new photographs, new jokes and completely ordinary days waiting ahead.
