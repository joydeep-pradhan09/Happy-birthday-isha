A Page
Just For You

No list to finish. No countdown to watch. Just one small page to say that your birthday deserves a little space of its own.

Keep being you.

I hope this next year gives you plenty of reasons to laugh, new places to discover, people who make ordinary days better, and enough unexpected moments to become good stories later.

And when another birthday arrives, I hope there are even more memories worth putting into a page like this.

— Joy
